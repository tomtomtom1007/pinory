Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the developer of Pinory ("we") handles your information in the Pinory travel journal app ("the app"). If this English version and the Japanese version differ, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Information we collect

  • What you enter: your display name, your travel and date records (destinations, places, plans, notes, packing lists, souvenirs, expenses, chats, letters, answers to questions and so on), and photos.
  • Account information: your email address if you create an account, or the identifier and email address provided by Apple if you use Sign in with Apple.
  • Where and when photos were taken: when you record from photos or drop a pin from a photo, the app reads this from the photo on your device and saves it as the place and time of your journey. Location and other metadata are removed from photos stored in the cloud.
  • A user identifier: an anonymous ID the app creates automatically for syncing.
  • Whether you have Pinory Plus. To confirm your subscription, we receive the App Store or Google Play proof of purchase (transaction number, product and expiry date). We never receive your payment details.

The app does not collect information for advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2. How we use it

  • To save your journal and sync it between your devices.
  • To share your journal with the travel companions you invite, and with your partner in Our Journal.
  • To create your account, sign you in, and move your journal to a new phone.
  • To show notifications from your device (countdowns, On this day, letters ready to open, and so on).
  • To provide Pinory Plus.
  • To answer your questions and look into problems.

3. Who can see your journal

A journey can be seen only by the members you invite with an invite code or invite link. Our Journal is shared with your partner. A secret date stays hidden from your partner until the day it opens.

Photos stored in the cloud are kept at long addresses that cannot be guessed. Anyone who has the address can see the photo, so please don't share photo addresses with others.

4. Services we use

The app uses the following services. Except where required by law, we do not share your personal information with third parties without your consent.

  • Supabase (storing your data and photos, sign-in). Data is stored on servers in Japan (Tokyo).
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases, place search, notifications), when you use Pinory on iPhone.
  • Google (Google Play subscriptions, place search, notifications), when you use Pinory on Android.
  • OpenFreeMap (maps). To show maps, your device connects to the map provider.
  • Place search services such as OpenStreetMap's Nominatim. We send the words you search for and, when searching nearby, an approximate location. We do not send anything that identifies you.

5. How long we keep it, and deleting it

Your journal is kept until you delete it or delete your account.

However, on the free plan, if you have not opened the app for two years, we may delete the photos stored in the cloud. About a month before, a notification will appear on your device. Opening the app keeps your photos for another two years. Your written records (itineraries, chats, letters and other text) are not deleted. This does not apply to Pinory Plus members, or to shared journeys while another member is still using the app.

You can delete your account and the data stored in the cloud, including your photo files, from the app's menu: Account, then Delete account. Photo files are removed within a day. In shared journeys, what other members wrote stays with them. A couple journal and its dates stay with your partner (the memories and letters you wrote are deleted).

If you leave a couple journal, the photos and words you wrote, your letters and your answers are hidden from your partner and deleted after 30 days. You can undo leaving during those 30 days. If you choose to leave your memories in the journal, they stay in your partner's journal.

6. Security

Connections are encrypted, and your data can be read and written only by you and the members of each journey.

7. Children

If you are a minor, please use the app with the consent of a parent or guardian.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the update on this page.
