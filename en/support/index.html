Questions

Will my journal stay if I change phones?
Create an account from the menu (Account) with your email or Apple ID. Sign in with the same account on your new iPhone, and your journal comes back. Without an account, your journal cannot be restored if you delete the app or change phones.

How do I invite travel companions or my partner?
From a journey, or from Our Journal's settings, you can send an invite code and link. They join by opening the link, or by entering the code in the app.

How do I cancel Pinory Plus?
Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Pinory. You keep Plus until the end of the current period.

I paid, but Plus isn't showing.
Please try "Restore purchases" on the Pinory Plus screen.

How long are my photos kept?
For as long as you use the app. On the free plan, if you don't open the app for two years, photos stored in the cloud may be deleted (we'll notify you about a month before). Simply opening the app keeps them. Pinory Plus members are not affected.

How do I delete my account and data?
From the menu, choose Account, then Delete account. This deletes your account and the data stored in the cloud.
